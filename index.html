Sources & Methodology

This roadmap synthesizes, in original wording, the publicly available guidance below. It does not copy article text or images.

  • The AWS News Blog announcement is the primary official source for the program's existence and reward tiers (7 / 14 / 21 badges).
  • Community Builder Center posts were used to consolidate a practical, day-by-day interpretation of the 21 badges.
  • Badge requirements below are summarized, not quoted, and are labeled by source type.
  • Phase ordering is a recommended workflow — AWS has not published an official required sequence.
  • Anything not explicitly confirmed by AWS is marked "Verification needed" rather than assumed.
